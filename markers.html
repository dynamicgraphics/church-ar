Print the Markers

Print at 100% scale (no "fit to page"). Each marker is a colored star matching its arrow, with the scannable code in the white center. Mount each star flat where its arrow should appear — on a wall at eye level works well. Bigger stars scan from farther away. Keep the white center clean and uncovered, and don't laminate with a glossy finish that causes glare. Each star is tied to its number in the Editor.
